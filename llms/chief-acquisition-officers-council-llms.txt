# Chief Acquisition Officers Council (CAOC)

> The Chief Acquisition Officers Council is a senior interagency forum of the U.S. federal
> government, established under Section 16 of the Office of Federal Procurement Policy Act
> (41 U.S.C. 1311). It brings together agency Chief Acquisition Officers, the Under Secretary
> of Defense for Acquisition and Sustainment, and Senior Procurement Executives to monitor and
> improve the federal acquisition system. The Council publishes its charter, membership,
> working-group output and acquisition guidance through Acquisition.GOV; it operates no
> developer program, no API, and no host of its own.
